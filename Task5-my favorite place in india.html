Famous Indian Foods

Introduction: Indian cuisine is famous for its spices, taste and variety. Every state has its own special food. North Indian food is rich and spicy, South Indian is light and healthy, and street food is loved by everyone.

Ordered List - 5 Famous Indian Dishes

  1. Biryani
  2. Butter Chicken
  3. Masala Dosa
  4. Chole Bhature
  5. Panipuri

Food Categories

  • North Indian
  • South Indian
  • Street Food
  • Desserts

Description

Biryani
Biryani is a rice dish made with spices, meat and vegetables. Famous in Hyderabad.
Masala Dosa
South Indian crispy dosa made from rice batter with potato filling.
Panipuri
Popular street food with crispy puris filled with spicy water.
